# Mara Heritage Bank — Q3 2025 Board Pack — Strategy

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This strategy document provides the Mara Heritage Bank Board with a concise, evidence-based assessment of the bank's strategic position as of Q3 2025. It is designed to facilitate informed discussions and decisions at the upcoming Q3 meeting regarding Q4 capital allocation, the digital transformation refresh, and the recalibration of our risk tolerance framework. We aim for each director to review this pack efficiently and arrive prepared to cast their votes on these critical strategic imperatives.

STRATEGIC POSITION & Q3 OVERVIEW
Mara Heritage Bank continues to navigate a dynamic market landscape, maintaining a resilient strategic posture through Q3 2025. Our core business lines have performed robustly, underpinned by strong client relationships and prudent market engagement. This quarter has seen progress across several strategic initiatives, yet also highlighted areas requiring concentrated focus as we plan for Q4 and beyond. Our strategic objectives remain centered on sustainable growth, operational excellence, and enhancing shareholder value, all while adapting to evolving regulatory and technological environments.

FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS
Q3 2025 financial performance has generally met internal targets, reflecting sound operational management and effective capital deployment. Key indicators suggest a stable financial foundation, providing the necessary runway for strategic investments and managing potential market fluctuations. Detailed financial reports are provided in supplementary materials, but the overarching picture supports our capacity for planned capital allocations.

RISK REVIEW & RECALIBRATION
The Chief Risk Officer's assessment indicates a clear understanding of our risk landscape. We continue to monitor the NPL trajectory, ensuring our hedging and provisioning strategies are appropriately aligned. Single-name concentration remains a focal point, with ongoing efforts to diversify and mitigate exposure. Operational risk has been meticulously tracked, revealing areas where process enhancements can further strengthen our control environment. These insights are fundamental to the board's decision on the risk-tolerance recalibration, ensuring it accurately reflects our current operating environment and strategic ambitions.

DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION UPDATE
Our ongoing digital transformation journey is a cornerstone of our future competitiveness. While significant progress has been made, the rapid pace of technological innovation and shifting customer expectations necessitate a strategic refresh. The proposed refresh will outline updated objectives, investment priorities, and key performance indicators to ensure our digital capabilities continue to drive efficiency, enhance customer experience, and open new avenues for growth. The board’s input and approval are crucial for aligning this strategy with our long-term vision.

PEOPLE & CULTURE
Our people remain our most vital asset. The CHRO's Q3 review highlights specific trends in attrition rates, prompting focused initiatives to understand and address underlying factors. Strategic attention is being placed on key-talent retention programs, recognizing that attracting and retaining top-tier professionals is paramount to executing our strategic objectives. We are committed to fostering an inclusive and high-performance culture that supports our strategic goals and employee well-being.

KEY DECISIONS FOR BOARD CONSIDERATION
Based on the strategic position outlined, the Board is requested to consider and vote on the following critical decisions:
*   Approval of the Q4 capital allocation plan, ensuring optimal deployment of resources for strategic growth and operational stability.
*   Endorsement of the proposed digital-transformation refresh, setting the strategic direction for our technological evolution.
*   Ratification of the risk-tolerance recalibration, aligning our risk appetite with current market realities and future strategic objectives.
