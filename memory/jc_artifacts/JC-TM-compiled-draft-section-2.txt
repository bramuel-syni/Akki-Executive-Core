# Mara Heritage Bank — Q3 2025 Board Pack — Performance

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This Q3 2025 Board Pack aims to Equip the board with a concise, evidence-based read on Mara Heritage Bank’s strategic position, enabling informed decisions on critical Q4 initiatives. Our analysis underscores a stable operational environment while highlighting key areas for strategic recalibration. This document is structured to facilitate swift comprehension, ensuring directors are prepared to vote on Q4 capital allocation, the digital-transformation refresh, and the risk-tolerance recalibration at the Q3 meeting.

STRATEGIC PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW

Mara Heritage Bank has maintained a robust strategic trajectory through Q3 2025, demonstrating resilience amidst evolving market conditions. Our core business lines continue to show steady performance, supporting the foundational stability required for strategic initiatives. This quarter’s performance metrics align with our long-term objectives, particularly in positioning the bank for significant advancements in digital capabilities and prudent capital deployment. The insights provided herein form the basis for upcoming decisions regarding our forward-looking capital structure and technological evolution.

PEOPLE & CULTURE

Q3 2025 saw attrition rates within anticipated ranges, with targeted initiatives in place to address specific areas. Key-talent retention remains a strategic priority, with focused development programs yielding positive outcomes. Efforts to foster a high-performance culture are ongoing, contributing to operational efficiency and innovation capacity. Our people strategy is designed to ensure Mara Heritage Bank possesses the talent necessary to execute its digital transformation and strategic objectives.

RISK POSITION

The bank’s risk profile for Q3 2025 indicates a managed environment. The NPL trajectory remains within acceptable bounds, reflecting a disciplined approach to credit origination and portfolio management. Single-name concentration continues to be diligently monitored and controlled, with no material shifts beyond established thresholds. Operational risk has seen an uptick in minor incidents; however, these have been promptly addressed through enhanced process controls and technology safeguards, with no significant impact on business continuity or financial performance.

KEY DECISION POINTS

Based on the performance outlined, the board is requested to consider and vote on three critical strategic imperatives:
1.  **Q4 Capital Allocation:** Review and approve the proposed capital allocation strategy for Q4, designed to optimize returns and support strategic growth initiatives.
2.  **Digital-Transformation Refresh:** Endorse the updated roadmap and investment plan for our digital transformation, ensuring alignment with market demands and competitive positioning.
3.  **Risk-Tolerance Recalibration:** Approve the proposed recalibration of our enterprise-wide risk tolerance framework, reflecting current market dynamics and strategic ambitions while maintaining prudent oversight.
