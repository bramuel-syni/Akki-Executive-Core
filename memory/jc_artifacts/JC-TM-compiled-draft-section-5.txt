# Mara Heritage Bank — Q3 2025 Board Pack — Governance

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This Q3 2025 Board Pack provides Mara Heritage Bank’s Board of Directors with a concise, evidence-based assessment of our strategic position. The aim is to Equip the Board with the necessary insights to facilitate informed decisions on critical matters queued for the upcoming Q3 meeting, specifically Q4 capital allocation, the digital-transformation refresh, and the recalibration of our risk tolerance. Our objective is to ensure each board member can review this pack in under 45 minutes and arrive prepared to cast decisive votes on these strategic imperatives.

STRATEGIC POSITION OVERVIEW
Mara Heritage Bank continues to navigate a dynamic market landscape, demonstrating resilience and strategic adaptability. Our Q3 performance reaffirms our commitment to sustainable growth while actively managing evolving macroeconomic factors and competitive pressures. We are proactively aligning our internal capabilities with market opportunities, focusing on robust financial health and client-centric innovation. This pack provides the foundational data and analyses supporting our ongoing strategic trajectory, emphasizing the critical linkages between capital deployment, technological advancement, and our comprehensive risk framework.

PEOPLE & CULTURE
Our human capital remains a cornerstone of Mara Heritage Bank’s success. Q3 data indicates a carefully managed attrition rate, with targeted interventions successfully stabilizing key talent segments. Proactive measures, including enhanced career development programs and competitive compensation adjustments, are being implemented to bolster key-talent retention across all critical business lines. The focus remains on fostering a high-performance culture that supports our strategic objectives, ensuring we attract, develop, and retain the talent necessary to execute our long-term vision.

RISK OVERVIEW
The bank’s risk profile remains within established parameters, with diligent oversight applied across all risk categories. The NPL trajectory shows a managed evolution, reflecting both our strategic growth initiatives and the prevailing economic climate. Targeted analysis of single-name concentration demonstrates diversification efforts are effectively mitigating potential exposure. Furthermore, operational risk management continues to be a priority, with ongoing enhancements to our control frameworks and incident response protocols to safeguard against emerging threats and ensure business continuity. These efforts are integral to maintaining a robust and resilient operational environment.

KEY DECISIONS FOR BOARD ACTION
At the upcoming Q3 meeting, the Board is requested to provide decisive guidance and approval on three critical strategic initiatives. First, a review and vote on the Q4 capital allocation strategy, ensuring optimal deployment of resources to support growth and maintain financial strength. Second, approval of the proposed digital-transformation refresh, which outlines key investments and initiatives to enhance our technological capabilities and competitive edge. Third, a recalibration of our risk tolerance framework, aligning it with current market realities and our evolving strategic ambitions. These decisions are vital to Mara Heritage Bank’s continued success and its ability to deliver sustainable value.
