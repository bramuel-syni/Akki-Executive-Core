# Mara Heritage Bank — Q3 2025 Board Pack — Executive Summary

INTRODUCTION
This Executive Summary for Mara Heritage Bank's Q3 2025 Board Pack is prepared to Equip the board with a concise, evidence-based read on the bank's strategic position. This overview is critical for enabling informed decisions on the capital, risk, and digital-transformation imperatives queued for the upcoming Q3 meeting.

STRATEGIC CONTEXT
Q3 2025 saw Mara Heritage Bank navigate a dynamic landscape, reinforcing its strategic priorities while adapting to evolving market conditions. This summary highlights key performance drivers and areas requiring board oversight, ensuring directors are fully equipped to address critical agenda items.

KEY DECISIONS FOR BOARD ACTION
The agenda for the Q3 meeting includes three pivotal decisions requiring board vote:
*   Approval of Q4 capital allocation.
*   Ratification of the digital-transformation refresh strategy.
*   Recalibration of the bank's risk tolerance framework.

PEOPLE & CULTURE
Our human capital strategy remains a cornerstone of our success. Q3 data indicates a manageable level of attrition, with targeted initiatives proving effective in mitigating broader impacts. Key-talent retention strategies continue to yield positive results, ensuring critical capabilities are preserved and developed internally. Further details on specific metrics and ongoing programs are available in the accompanying CHRO report.

RISK MANAGEMENT
The bank's risk profile in Q3 reflects a disciplined approach amidst a complex operating environment. The NPL trajectory is being closely monitored, with current trends aligning with expectations. Analysis of single-name concentration risks demonstrates robust mitigation strategies are in place, while vigilance around operational risk (op-risk) remains paramount, supported by ongoing enhancements to our control environment. The Chief Risk Officer's full report provides comprehensive insights into these areas.

FORWARD LOOK
The insights contained within this executive summary and the broader board pack are designed to facilitate efficient deliberation, enabling the board to confidently cast votes on Q4 capital allocation, the digital-transformation refresh, and the risk-tolerance recalibration, steering Mara Heritage Bank towards continued strategic success.
